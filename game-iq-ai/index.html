The science of the game

Built on soccer biomechanics

Six mechanics categories

  • Running — acceleration, deceleration, stride, balance, change of direction.
  • Kicking — support foot, hip-knee-ankle sequencing, contact and follow-through.
  • Receiving — body shape, first-touch angle, control under pressure.
  • Movement science — braking, re-acceleration, turning, posture in duels.
  • Ball physics — pass speed, trajectory and spin, where the footage allows.
  • Tactical mechanics — scanning, support angles, spacing, pressing triggers.
Every section carries a High / Medium / Low confidence label. We never claim what the camera can't see — and even a 30-second clip returns useful analysis.
