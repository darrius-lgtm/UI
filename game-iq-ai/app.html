Movement observations

On the ball
  • 47 touches, mostly in the central channel between halfway and the attacking third.
  • Receives side-on with an open body on 7 of 10 receptions — well above age benchmark.
  • When pressed from behind, releases the ball in under 1 second and rarely turns — 2 of 9 attempts to play forward off the half-turn.
Off the ball
  • Constant scanning: 4.1 looks per 10 seconds before receiving in midfield.
  • Good covering runs when the right back pushed up — recovered position within 6 seconds on average.
  • Tends to drift toward the ball in attack, closing the passing lane a teammate needs. Happened 5 times in the first half.

Strengths

Keep building
  • Elite scanning habits for the age group. Checks both shoulders before nearly every reception. This is the foundation of a high-level midfielder.
  • Composure in tight central areas. Only 3 lost possessions from 47 touches despite a congested midfield.
  • Defensive engine. Top-three sprint efforts on the team in defensive transition; never left the midfield short.

Improvement areas

Priority focus
  • Playing forward under pressure. The safe pass is the default. The next level requires risking the line-breaking ball when it's on.
  • Weak-foot confidence. 5 of 6 left-footed passes were short and under-hit. Defenders will start forcing Jordan left.
  • Pressing body shape. Approaches the ball-carrier straight-on, allowing escapes to either side. Curved runs would cut the pitch in half.

Summaries

Parent summary

Jordan had a genuinely strong game. The habit of looking around before getting the ball — something most kids this age don't do — is Jordan's superpower, and it showed in the assist for the first goal. The one thing holding Jordan back is caution: when an exciting forward pass is available, Jordan usually picks the safe one. That's normal at this stage and very coachable. This month, celebrate the brave passes even when they don't come off. The drills above take about 30 minutes, three times a week, and only need a ball and a wall.

Coach summary

Profile: progressing 8 with high scan frequency (4.1/10s) and clean reception data, but a conservative pass map — 81% of completions lateral or backward. Recommend rondo variants rewarding split passes, and constraints games where lateral passes are capped. Out of possession, pressing approach is flat; work curved-run pressing with cover shadow on the near passing lane. Transition recognition is ahead of the age curve — consider trialing Jordan as the single pivot in a 4-3-3 to force forward distribution.

Player summary

Jordan — that pass in the 8th minute? That's who you are. You see the field better than almost anyone your age because you check your shoulders constantly. Keep that forever. Here's your one big challenge: be braver. Three times this match a teammate was open ahead of you and you played sideways. You have the vision — now trust it. Your mission for the next game: two forward passes that break a line. We're counting. And get that left foot working — 25 minutes against a wall this week.
